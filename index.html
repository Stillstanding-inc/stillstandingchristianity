Still Standing Christianity

The ministry heart of Still Standing — God's hand on His chosen one, kept standing.

"Therefore, my dear brothers and sisters, stand firm. Let nothing move you." — 1 Corinthians 15:58

The vision

God gave James Bosarge the vision in the year 2000, at 9211 Four Mile Road in Irvington, Alabama — and he has carried it ever since, through every season that tried to take him down. The work has been built in the open since then, and its verified public record stands from July 23, 2025.

Still Standing Christianity is the testimony that God keeps His own standing — and the hand reached back for anybody else who needs to stand back up. Whatever knocked you down is not the end of your story. That is the whole message, and it has never changed.

How the ministry reaches out

  • The books — the Still Standing trilogy, The Proclamations, The Chronicles, and the True North Star curriculum, written out of a real life God carried through
  • The music — songs of faith and standing, on the Loyalty Respect Records label
  • The Help Line — free, verified food and help for anybody who needs it, and a Coming Home arm for people coming out of prison
  • Hope in the Halls — prayer and presence for people in hospital halls
  • All Arms Linked — survivors standing with survivors
